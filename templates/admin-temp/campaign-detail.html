Campaign Description

Raju is a 2-year-old Indian Pariah Dog rescued from near the Bandra-Kurla Complex in Mumbai on 28 May 2025. He was found with severe injuries to his hind legs, likely from being hit by a vehicle, and was also severely malnourished.

Our veterinary team has assessed that Raju requires at least 3 months of intensive care, including surgery on his right hind leg, physiotherapy, and a full vaccination and deworming schedule. The campaign funds will cover his complete medical treatment, feed, and shelter costs — and ultimately, his adoption screening and placement.

₹33,000 still needed — 20 days remaining. Please help us get there!
