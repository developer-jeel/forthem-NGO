Our Programs

What We Do

Infrastructure

Community Toilet Blocks

Building and maintaining hygienic toilet facilities in slum settlements where no public sanitation exists — reducing open defecation and related health risks.

Education

Hygiene Education Workshops

Conducting interactive workshops in schools and community centres on handwashing, menstrual hygiene, safe drinking water, and disease prevention.

Waste Management

Solid Waste Segregation

Setting up colour-coded waste segregation stations, partnering with PMC, and training households in dry-wet waste separation and composting.

Impact Story

"Children now wash hands before eating — something they never did before."

Headmistress Sunita Mahadik of Zilla Parishad School, Wadgaon, on the transformation her school saw after three Unke Liye hygiene workshops.

Post-workshop assessments show a 78% increase in consistent handwashing behaviour among students — and a measurable drop in illness-related absences.

Our Approach

How We Run a Sanitation Program

Community Survey

Door-to-door assessment of sanitation gaps and needs.

Stakeholder Buy-In

Work with local leaders, PMC, and schools for permissions.

Infrastructure Build

Construct toilet blocks with local labour and quality materials.

Hygiene Training

Interactive workshops at schools, women's groups, and RWAs.

Kit Distribution

Hand out hygiene kits: soap, sanitiser, reusable pads, toothbrush.

Follow-Up Audit

3-month and 1-year audits to measure behaviour change.
