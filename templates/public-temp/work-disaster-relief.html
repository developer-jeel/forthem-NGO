Our Protocol

3-Phase Disaster Response

From emergency rescue to long-term community rebuilding — we stay until recovery is complete.

🚨

Phase 1 — Immediate

Deployed within 4 hours: rescue boats, ropes, life jackets, first-aid teams, animal rescue kits, and emergency food packets.

  • Rescue operations (human + animal)
  • Medical first aid on-site
  • Emergency food + water distribution
  • Shelter blanket kits
🏕️

Phase 2 — Relief

Days 2–14: sustained relief camps, medical check-ups, psychological first aid, sanitation kit distribution, and animal care tents.

  • Relief camp management
  • Daily ration distribution
  • Veterinary care for livestock
  • Counselling sessions
🏗️

Phase 3 — Rehabilitation

Weeks 3–12: livelihood support, house repair grants, animal restocking, school reopening drives, and psychological rehabilitation.

  • Cash-for-work programs
  • Home repair material kits
  • School supply restoration
  • Animal-owner reunification

Community Preparedness

We Train Communities Before Disasters Strike

Prevention saves more lives than response. We run disaster preparedness workshops in flood-prone and drought-prone districts — training community volunteers in first aid, evacuation routes, and animal-handling during disasters.

Certified 2-day workshops training community volunteers in CPR, wound management, rescue breathing, and triage protocols for mass casualty events.
Training farmers and pet owners in moving, calming, and providing first-aid to animals during floods, fires, and earthquakes — reducing livestock loss significantly.
Conducted in partnership with NDRF in 24 flood-prone villages across Kolhapur, Sangli, and Pune districts.
