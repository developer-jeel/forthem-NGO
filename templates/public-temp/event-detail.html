✅ Open for RSVP 📅 September 21, 2025 🐾 Adoption

Monthly Adoption Camp
Find Your Forever Friend

Meet over 30 rescued dogs, cats, and small animals. Bring your family, ask questions, and take a new best friend home.

Date
📅 Sunday, 21 September 2025
Time
⏰ 10:00 AM — 4:00 PM
Venue
📍 D-Mart Parking, Sinhagad Rd
Entry
🎟️ Free (no registration required)

About This Event

Our monthly adoption camp is the perfect opportunity to meet our rescued animals face-to-face. 30+ healthy, vaccinated, and sterilised dogs, cats, puppies, and kittens will be present, each with their own story and personality.

Our experienced team will be on-site to match you with the right companion, answer questions about adoption formalities, and explain ongoing post-adoption support. Same-day adoption is possible if paperwork is complete.

Event Agenda

1

10:00 AM — Animal Meet & Greet

Walk through our animal tents, interact with the animals, and ask handlers questions.

2

11:30 AM — Adoption Counselling Desk

Our team helps you understand care requirements and choose the right animal for your lifestyle.

3

12:00 PM — Vet Q&A Session

Dr. Priya Joshi answers questions about pet health, vaccination schedules, and nutrition.

4

1:00 PM — Adoption Formalities

Fill forms, pay adoption fee (if applicable), receive starter kit, and take your pet home.

5

2:00 – 4:00 PM — Photo Zone & Community Corner

Celebrate new adoptions! Photo booth with props, sponsor meet, and donations stall.

What to Bring

  • Government-issued ID (Aadhaar / Passport) for adoption paperwork
  • Previous adoption / pet care experience (optional, helps with matching)
  • Cloth bags for supplies — no single-use plastic at our events
  • Your excitement and an open heart!
