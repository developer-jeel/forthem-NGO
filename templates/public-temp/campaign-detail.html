Bruno's Story

On June 3rd, 2025, our rescue team received a distress call from a resident near Wakad, Pune. A street dog had been hit by a speeding vehicle and was unable to move. We found Bruno — a young male Indie, approximately 3 years old — with a compound fracture of his right hind leg.

Dr. Kavita Nair at our partner clinic assessed him and confirmed that orthopaedic surgery with a stainless steel implant was the only way to restore leg function. Without it, amputation would be necessary.

The surgery costs ₹35,000 — beyond our emergency budget. We are raising this amount through this campaign so Bruno can walk, run, and play again — and eventually be adopted into a loving home.
