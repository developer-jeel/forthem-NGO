4. Animal Adoption

Adoption from Unke Liye is conditional upon:

  • Submission and approval of a written adoption application
  • A home visit or virtual assessment at Unke Liye's discretion
  • Payment of the applicable adoption contribution fee
  • Signing of an Adoption Agreement document

Adopters agree to provide appropriate care, food, shelter, veterinary treatment, and a safe environment to the adopted animal. Unke Liye reserves the right to reclaim an animal if there is evidence of neglect, abuse, or conditions materially different from what was disclosed in the adoption application.

Adoption contribution fees are non-refundable once the animal is in the adopter's care. In the event that an adoption does not work out within the first 30 days, we will accept the animal back and work to find a new placement.
