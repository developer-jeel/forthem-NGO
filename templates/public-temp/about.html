About Unke Liye

Born from urgency, built on love — the story of a small team that refused to look away.

🔭

Our Vision

A Maharashtra — and eventually an India — where no animal suffers on the street, no forest is razed without a replacement, no community faces a disaster alone, and where every person feels a responsibility to care for the living world around them.

We believe care is contagious. When one person acts, five follow. We are building that culture — one rescue, one tree, one drive, one disaster response at a time.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide rapid, compassionate, and professional rescue and relief across four areas — Animal Healthcare, Environmental Restoration, Community Sanitation, and Disaster Response — with full transparency, zero bureaucracy, and deep community rooting.

Every rupee donated, every hour volunteered, and every report submitted through us generates a direct, documented, and measurable impact on a real life.

Our Story

It Started with One Dog and a Midnight Phone Call

In 2015, Priya Kulkarni — a software engineer with no NGO experience — received a frantic call from a neighbour about an injured dog hit by a car near Hadapsar. She called every rescue number she could find. No one picked up. She went herself, wrapped the dog in her dupatta, and drove to three different vets before finding one who would treat a street animal.

That dog survived. Priya didn't stop. Three friends joined. They named the effort Unke Liye — "For Them" — because it was always about the ones who couldn't speak for themselves.

Nine years later, we have a team of 47 staff, 340+ active volunteers, a full veterinary clinic, a shelter housing 80+ animals, and operations spanning animal rescue, environmental drives, community sanitation, and disaster relief across Maharashtra.

But we still pick up every call.

Our Journey

Nine Years of Showing Up

2015
Founded — First Rescue
Unke Liye registered as an NGO in Pune. First veterinary tie-up with Dr. Anand Deshpande clinic. 12 rescues in Year 1.
2016
First Vaccination Camp
Partnered with Pune Municipal Corporation to run Maharashtra's first free street animal vaccination camp — 800 animals vaccinated in one weekend.
2017
Green Pillar Launched
Expanded beyond animals — first tree plantation drive in Sinhagad Road hills. 1,200 trees planted. Partnerships with 6 schools.
2018
First Disaster Response
Responded to Kerala floods — 47 volunteers deployed, 1,200 families supported. This defined the Disaster Relief pillar permanently.
2019
Shelter & Clinic Opened
Inaugurated our permanent shelter in Hadapsar — capacity 80 animals, full veterinary clinic, ICU unit, and adoption center.
2021
COVID Relief — 14,000 Meals
Distributed 14,000+ dry ration kits across Pune's slums during lockdown. Partnered with 3 corporate CSR funds for emergency relief.
2023
Sanitation Pillar Formalized
Launched structured Sanitation & Cleanliness pillar. 120+ drives, 4,500 kg of waste removed from waterways in first year.
2024
Maharashtra NGO of the Year
Awarded Maharashtra State Social Welfare Department's NGO of the Year. 14,800+ animals rescued since inception. We don't plan to stop.
What Guides Us

Our Core Values

💛

Compassion First

Every decision starts with the question: what is best for the being who cannot advocate for itself?

🔍

Radical Transparency

Every rupee is documented. Every report is public. We believe donors deserve to see exactly how their trust is spent.

⚡

Speed of Response

Emergencies don't wait. We aim for under 45-minute response in our primary zones and activate disaster teams within hours.

🤝

Community Rooting

We don't parachute in and out. We train local volunteers, build local systems, and ensure every solution outlasts our presence.
