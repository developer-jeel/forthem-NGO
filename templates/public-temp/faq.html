💰 Donations
Yes. Unke Liye is registered under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. All donations qualify for a 50% tax deduction. You will receive your 80G certificate via email within 7 working days of your donation. PAN number is required to issue the certificate.
We accept all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and digital wallets. Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your payment details.
The minimum donation we accept is ₹100. There is no maximum limit. Every amount genuinely makes a difference — ₹100 can cover a day's food for a recovering animal.
82% of all donations go directly toward program activities (rescue operations, vet care, environment drives, disaster relief). 10% covers administrative costs (staff, infrastructure), and 8% covers fundraising costs. Full annual financial statements and utilization reports are published on our Transparency page.
Yes! Monthly giving is our most impactful form of support — it allows us to plan rescue operations and maintain continuous programs. You can set up a monthly donation on our Donate page by selecting "Monthly." You can pause or cancel anytime by emailing us.
Yes, we do tribute donations. Please email us at donate@unkeliye.org with the details and we'll issue a special acknowledgment certificate in the name of the person being honored.
🙋 Volunteering
Fill out our Volunteer Registration Form online. Our coordinator will contact you within 3 working days to schedule an orientation session. There is no minimum commitment — we welcome people who can only do 3 hours a month.
No experience is needed for most roles. We provide full training before any field assignment. For specialized roles (veterinary assistance, medical support), relevant qualifications are appreciated but not always mandatory.
Yes. After completing 20+ volunteer hours with us, you receive an official Unke Liye Volunteer Certificate signed by our Founder and stamped with our registration seal. Many volunteers use this for college, job applications, and CSR requirements.
Yes! We have remote volunteer roles: social media management, content writing, graphic design, fundraising outreach, data management, and donor communication. Apply and mention your city — we'll match you to a remote role.
🐾 Adoption & Fostering
Browse our Adopt page to find an animal. Submit an online application, meet the animal at our shelter, and if it's a match — complete the paperwork and adoption fee. Every adoption includes the animal's full vaccination record, microchip ID, sterilization certificate, and a 30-day follow-up call from our team.
Yes. Adoption fees range from ₹500 to ₹2,000 depending on the animal. This covers final health check, deworming, microchipping, and paperwork. It is not a commercial fee — it's a small contribution to their care costs.
Fostering means providing a temporary home for an animal — usually 2 weeks to 3 months — while we find them a permanent family. We provide food, medical care, and support. You provide love, shelter, and time. Many fosters become permanent adopters. Visit our Foster page to learn more.
Yes! Our Sponsor an Animal program lets you support a specific animal's care with a monthly contribution. You receive regular updates and photos. It's perfect for people who can't adopt or foster but want a personal connection.
🚨 Rescue Reporting
Call our 24/7 hotline at +91 88888 00000, or fill out our online rescue form. Provide the location (with landmark), animal type, and condition. We dispatch the nearest team immediately. If the situation is life-threatening, call first — don't wait to fill the form.
Our primary rescue zone is Pune and immediate surrounding areas (within 60km). For other Maharashtra locations, we coordinate with partner NGOs. For all other states during disasters, we deploy our disaster relief team. Report any case to us — we'll do our best or refer you to the right organization.
Yes. After submitting a report, you'll receive a reference number. You can track the rescue status on our Rescue Status Tracker, or call us with your reference number for an update.
🆘 Disaster Relief
We prioritize disasters based on: scale of impact, gap in existing relief (are government agencies already there?), our existing volunteer presence in the region, and availability of funds. We focus on being where we can add the most value, not just where the cameras are.
Check the "Emergency Response Volunteer" option on our Volunteer form. You'll be required to attend a one-day emergency preparedness orientation. After that, you'll be on our emergency roster and contacted when needed.
📋 General & Legal
Yes. Unke Liye is registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 with Registration No. MAH/2015/1234 (Maharashtra). We hold FCRA registration (for foreign contributions), 80G certification (for tax deductions), and 12A certification. All documents are available on our Transparency page.
Our main shelter, clinic, and office are in Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra. We also have operations across Pune city, Nashik, and temporary field offices during disaster operations in other states.
