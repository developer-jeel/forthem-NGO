Zero Cost to You

Everything We Provide

You open your door. We handle every expense. Our foster program is truly free for hosts.

🥩

Premium Food & Bowls

Nutritionist-approved kibble and wet food delivered to your door monthly — tailored to the animal's age and health needs.

🏥

Full Medical & Vet Care

All clinic visits, vaccinations, deworming, medications, emergency surgeries — 100% covered. You never pay a rupee.

🧸

Bedding, Crates & Toys

Soft beds, transport crates, leashes, harnesses, toys, and grooming kits — delivered before your foster guest arrives.

📞

24 / 7 Expert Helpline

Reach our experienced animal behaviourists and coordinators any hour — for advice, reassurance, or emergency support.

🗓️

Flexible Duration

Short 2-week emergency stays to 2-month rehabilitation periods — we match the duration to your availability and lifestyle.

🏡

Adoption Matchmaking

We screen adopters, arrange home visits, and handle all paperwork. Foster parents get first priority for permanent adoption.
