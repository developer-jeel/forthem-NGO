While You Wait for the Team

  • Do not try to handle or lift an injured wild animal — you could hurt yourself or the animal further.
  • Keep the animal calm by speaking softly and not crowding around it.
  • Block traffic if the animal is on a road — use your vehicle, hazard lights, or bystanders.
  • Keep the area clear of other dogs or aggressive animals.
  • Cover the animal lightly with a cloth if it's raining or cold.
  • Send your live location to our team when they call you.
