How It Works

From Distress Call to Safe Recovery

📞

1. You Call or Report

Via hotline, app, or our website. You give us location, condition, and photos if possible.

🚐

2. Team Dispatched

Our nearest rescue unit — with trained staff and equipment — is dispatched within minutes.

🏥

3. Immediate Care

On-site stabilization, transport to our clinic or partner vet. Treatment begins immediately.

🏡

4. Rehab & Adoption

Recovery, socialization, spay/neuter, vaccination — then a forever home or a return to their territory.

Our Programs

What We Do for Animals

🚑

24/7 Emergency Rescue

Round-the-clock rescue operations for injured, sick, trapped, or distressed street animals. Three rescue vans on standby across Pune.

🏥

Full Veterinary Clinic

Our Hadapsar clinic handles 40+ cases weekly — surgeries, intensive care, chronic disease management, and specialist referrals.

💉

Vaccination Camps

Free street animal vaccination camps monthly across Pune — rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and more. No appointment needed.

✂️

Sterilization Program

Humane and ethical ABC (Animal Birth Control) drives in partnership with PMC — reducing population without cruelty.

🏠

Shelter & Boarding

Our 80-animal shelter provides medical boarding, recovery space, and socialization before adoption placement.

🐾

Adoption & Foster

Connecting recovered animals with loving families. Adoption includes vaccination record, microchipping, and a follow-up call at 30 days.
