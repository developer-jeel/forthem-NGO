Our Programs

What We Do for the Environment

Four focused tracks — each measurable, each impactful, each rooted in community action.

🌱 Sahyadri saplings, 2024

Urban Tree Planting Drive

We plant 5,000+ trees every monsoon season in Pune's parks, school campuses, and roadside green belts — partnering with PMC, PCMC, and Forest Department.

Each tree is GPS-tagged. Our volunteers monitor survival rate quarterly — currently at 84%, above the national average of 60%.

💧 Mula-Mutha, 2024

Mula-Mutha River Cleanup

Monthly cleanup drives along Pune's Mula and Mutha rivers — removing garbage, plastic, and industrial waste with 50–200 volunteers each session.

We've removed 18 tons of plastic waste and 40+ tons of total debris over the past two years — measurably improving water quality metrics.

♻️ Plastic-free campaign

Zero Plastic Initiative

Free distribution of cloth bags, awareness campaigns in markets, and partnerships with local businesses to eliminate single-use plastic in 25 Pune neighbourhoods.

🦋 Biodiversity patches
