Bruno's Comeback — The Dog Who Refused to Give Up

Found broken on a highway. Rebuilt by love, science, and community. Bruno is now running laps in his forever home in Kothrud, Pune.

When our rescue team found Bruno on a deserted stretch of the Pune-Solapur highway, he was barely conscious — his right hind leg bent at an impossible angle.

It was 2:30 AM on June 3rd, 2025. Volunteer Vikram Joshi had received a tip from a truck driver who had spotted the injured dog and stopped just long enough to call our helpline. Twenty minutes later, our duty team was on-site.

The vet's diagnosis was unambiguous: compound fracture of the right femur, caused by high-speed vehicle impact. Without surgery within 48 hours, the leg would need amputation. The cost — ₹35,000 — was beyond our emergency budget.

"I had never crowd-funded for an animal before. But Bruno's eyes — even in that pain — there was something so full of life. I shared the campaign at 8 AM. By noon, we had 50 donors." — Rahul Nimkar, Rapid Response Coordinator

The Community That Saved Him

The campaign went live on August 10th. Within 4 days, 142 donors had collectively raised ₹27,300 — enough to proceed with surgery. The remaining gap was filled by a corporate donor who had followed Bruno's story on Instagram.

The surgery was performed on August 14th by Dr. Kavita Nair. A titanium implant was placed, and Bruno was transferred to a recovery ward at our sanctuary.

Priya's Family — Bruno's Forever Home

Priya Kulkarni had initially applied to foster Bruno during his recovery. "I just wanted to help for a few weeks," she laughs. "But by week two, he had become part of the family."

Six weeks after surgery, Bruno walked out of his foster home as a full member of the Kulkarni family. He now has his own bed, a toy basket, and a routine of morning walks in Kothrud's lanes — without a trace of a limp.

"I fostered Bruno for 5 weeks. By the end, the idea of him going anywhere else was unthinkable. We just signed the adoption papers last week. He's family."

Priya Kulkarni Adoptive parent, Kothrud, Pune
