Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 January 2024  ·  Effective Date: 1 January 2024
Questions? Contact: privacy@unkeliye.org

1. Introduction

Unke Liye ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals who interact with our website, donate to our causes, volunteer with us, or use our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

By using the Unke Liye website (unkeliye.org) or submitting information to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Donations: Name, email, phone, address, PAN number (for 80G certificates), payment information.
  • Volunteering: Name, email, phone, city, skills, availability, and motivation.
  • Adoption/Foster: Personal details, home situation, references.
  • Rescue Reports: Location, animal description, contact details, photos.
  • Newsletter: Email address only.
  • Contact Form: Name, email, phone, message.

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device information.
  • Pages visited, time spent, referral source.
  • Cookies (see Section 6 below).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing donations and issuing 80G certificates.
  • Coordinating volunteer activities and rescue operations.
  • Sending impact updates, receipts, and newsletters (with your consent).
  • Responding to contact and rescue report inquiries.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Legal compliance and regulatory reporting.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Payment Security

All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Unke Liye does not store your full card, UPI, or bank account details. All payment data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL.

5. Data Retention

Donor records are retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law. Volunteer records are retained for 3 years post-activity. Rescue reports are retained for 2 years. Newsletter subscriptions are maintained until you unsubscribe.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies (for site functionality) and analytics cookies (Google Analytics, privacy-respecting) to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function correctly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, withdraw consent for marketing communications, and file a complaint with appropriate authorities. Contact us at privacy@unkeliye.org to exercise these rights.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Razorpay (payments), Google Analytics (website analytics), Mailchimp or similar (newsletter). Each has their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email to registered donors and volunteers. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
