Cyclone Relief 2026:
Rebuild After the Storm

Support coastal villages and rural communities devastated by Cyclone Michaung. We are distributing food, restoring clean water, erecting temporary shelters, and rescuing displaced animals.

Emergency Rescue Response

Why This Campaign Matters

On August 10, 2026, Cyclone Michaung made landfall along the coastal tracts of western India, packing winds up to 140 km/h. Heavy rains and storm surges flooded rural lowlands, sweeping away mud houses, contaminating open drinking water wells, and displacing thousands of families and local street animals.

Unke Liye’s initial response team arrived on site within 12 hours. The immediate needs are critical: safe hot meals, water purification kits, emergency medical care, and dry temporary shelters. Without this intervention, water-borne illnesses pose a severe risk to displaced survivors in temporary camps.

Beyond instant food relief, we are committed to helping families stand back on their feet. Fostering a cycle of recovery means helping rebuild damaged roof structures, setting up secure enclosures for rescued farm animals, and delivering long-term rehabilitation support.
